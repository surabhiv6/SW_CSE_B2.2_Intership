Viewport: visible area of a webpage. It is the rectangular region where the browser actually displays the content.

CSS units: the measurement units used in CSS to define the size, position, and other dimensions of elements.

1. Absolute units: These units are fixed and do not change based on the context or screen size. Examples include: px(pixels), in(inches), cm(centimeters), mm(millimeters), pt(points), pc(picas). 2. Relative units: These units are relative to another value, such as the parent element's size or the viewport size. Examples include: em(relative to the font-size of the element), rem(relative to the font-size of the root element), vw(relative to 1% of the viewport's width), vh(relative to 1% of the viewport's height), vmin(relative to 1% of the viewport's smaller dimension), vmax(relative to 1% of the viewport's larger dimension),zero value(margin is 0).
